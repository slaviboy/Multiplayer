# Goal Heads (games/football)

One-on-one head football: kicks, headers and lobs. Play against the CPU (easy,
normal, hard), two players on one keyboard, or online with a friend through an
invite link or QR code.

The art and sounds are the original GoalHeads.io resources (GamesMrkt), which the
repo owner has the rights to. The game logic is new: the original is a Unity
WebGL build that only plays online through its developer's servers (Photon
Quantum, Nakama), so it could not be played peer to peer. Sizes, positions and
animation curves were taken from the original scene, prefabs and animation clips.

## Controls

| | Alone | Left player (two on one keyboard) | Right player |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D or ← / → | A / D | ← / → |
| Jump | W, ↑ or Space | W | ↑ |
| Shot (low kick) | T or K | T | K |
| Aerial (high kick) | Y or L | Y | L |

Esc or P pauses (not online). Phones and tablets get on-screen buttons. Headers
happen when the ball meets a head: the face sends it forward in a loop, the top
straight up, the back of the head backwards.

## Power-ups

After 20–30 seconds of play a fire icon and an ice icon float high over the pitch
(each at its own random time and place; they come back 12–22 s after being taken,
and fade after 12 s). When the ball hits one after a player touched it (kick,
header or any touch):

- **Fire** is for that player: for 10 s their next shot or header is a fireball,
  1.5× faster and flatter, which goes through the other player and knocks them
  over for 1.5 s.
- **Ice** is against the other player: frozen in an ice block for 3 s (it cracks
  in three stages); every key press breaks it a little sooner.

They can be switched off with the Power-ups option. The ice blocks, shards, fire
texture and sounds are the original's boosters. Where and when icons appear comes
from a seed in the match state, so online both players see the same ones.

## Files

- `js/sim.js` – the match: physics, kicks, headers, goals, clock, golden goal.
  Deterministic (fixed 40 Hz ticks, only exactly rounded maths), which online play
  relies on; a unit test checks it never uses the clock, `Math.random` or trig.
- `js/ai.js` – the CPU player. It presses keys like a person, seeded.
- `js/render.js` – drawing on the canvas, animations, effects, scoreboard.
- `js/ui.js`, `js/main.js` – screens and the app; `js/input.js` keyboard and touch.
- `js/online.js`, `js/lockstep.js` – online play (see below).
- `js/roster.js` – outfits: any head with any shirt and boots.
- `js/catalog.js`, `assets/` – generated, see below.

## Online

The host (left player) chooses a player and gets a link and QR code; the friend
opens it, chooses, and presses Ready; the host's pitch and match length are used.
The browsers connect directly over WebRTC (signalling through public Nostr relays,
`shared/online/`). Both run the same simulation in lockstep and send each other
only the keys pressed, a few ticks ahead to hide the delay; every second they
compare a hash of the match. A reload resumes the session; the match in progress
is abandoned. Chat appears as bubbles over the players' heads.

## Assets

`assets/` and `js/catalog.js` are generated by `node scripts/football-assets.mjs`
from the AssetRipper export of the original build, kept outside git in
`reference/football-goalheads/` (the original Unity build itself is in
`reference/football-unity-build/`). The script resizes and re-encodes the images
as WebP, copies the chosen sounds (AAC in `.m4a`) and picks each head's neck
colour from its skin tone. The font is Lilita One (SIL Open Font License), close
to the original's Bolden One, which only exists as a texture atlas in the build.

## Tests

`tests/unit/football.test.mjs` (simulation, CPU, lockstep, outfits, assets) runs
with `npm test`; `tests/e2e/football.test.mjs` plays in Chrome, including an
online match between a desktop and a phone (needs internet for the relays).
