Playing online

  1. 1

    Pick your character

    Open a game and choose Play online.

  2. 2

    Send the link

    Copy it, share it, or let your friend scan the QR code.

  3. 3

    Play together

    Your friend gets the other character. The connection is direct between your browsers.

Online play uses WebRTC. Public Nostr relays only help the two browsers find each other.
